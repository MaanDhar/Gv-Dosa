Our Story

Where the GriddleTells Its Story

GV Dosa was born from a deep reverence for South Indian home cooking. Every recipe we serve has travelled through generations — fermented overnight, ground by hand, tempered with care. We bring the honest flavours of Tamil Nadu straight to your table.

From the first ladle on the griddle to the last drop of sambar, every detail is intentional. No shortcuts, no compromise — only the pure taste of tradition.
